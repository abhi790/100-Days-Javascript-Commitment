Tiger Information

The tiger is the largest wild cat in the world. It is known for its orange fur with black stripes and powerful body. Tigers are carnivores and mainly hunt deer, wild boar, and other animals.

Tigers usually live in forests, grasslands, and mangrove swamps. India has the largest population of wild tigers in the world. They are important for maintaining the balance of the ecosystem.

Unfortunately, tigers are endangered due to habitat loss and poaching. Wildlife conservation programs and national parks help protect them.
