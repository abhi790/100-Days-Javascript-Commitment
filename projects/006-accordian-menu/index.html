Q1. What is your favorite animal?

My favorite animal is the elephant. Elephants are intelligent, social, and known for their strong memory. They live in groups and care deeply for their families.

Q2. Why do people visit zoos?

People visit zoos to see animals from different parts of the world. Zoos also help in animal conservation and educate visitors about wildlife and nature.

Q3. What do lions usually eat?

Lions are carnivores, which means they eat meat. They usually hunt animals like deer, zebras, and buffalo.

Q4. Why are forests important for animals?

Forests provide animals with food, shelter, and a safe place to live. Many species depend on forests for survival.

Q5. What makes pandas special?

Pandas are special because of their unique black and white fur and their diet of bamboo. They are also considered a symbol of wildlife conservation.
